Chant on a home speaker

Ashtotra sends its prayers to your speaker over Wi-Fi (AirPlay) or Bluetooth. No account with Amazon or Google is needed.

1. Choose your speaker

Open any prayer and tap the speaker button next to Listen. HomePod, newer Echo models and other AirPlay speakers appear in the list.

2. Echo or Google Nest by Bluetooth

Echo: say “Alexa, pair”, then choose your Echo in iPhone Settings › Bluetooth.
Google Nest or Home: say “Hey Google, Bluetooth pairing”, then choose it in Settings › Bluetooth.

3. Start at a set time

In the Shortcuts app choose Automation › Time of Day, then add the Ashtotra action Play today's prayer, Start my prayers or Play a prayer. Choose Run Immediately. Or just say “Hey Siri, start my prayers in Ashtotra”. Keep your iPhone nearby on the same Wi-Fi.
