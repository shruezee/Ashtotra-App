Ashtotra

108 sacred names, in your script · iPhone & iPad

Read and chant the Ashtottara Shatanamavali, the 108 names of Ganesha, Shiva, Lakshmi and Saraswati, in the script you read best. Chant one name at a time with a gentle mala of 108 beads, and pick up exactly where you left off.

Features

Six ways to readEasy English letters, scholarly IAST, Devanagari, Telugu, Kannada and Gujarati.
Chant modeOne name at a time, large and calm, with a 108-bead mala and a gentle tap with each name.
Never lose your placeStop anytime; Ashtotra remembers where you were in each list.
For every eyeFollows your text size, works with VoiceOver, and looks good in dark mode.
Carefully checkedEvery list has exactly 108 names, checked against more than one source.
PrivateNo ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.
