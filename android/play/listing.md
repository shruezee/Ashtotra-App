## Full description

Ashtotra is a calm companion for daily Hindu prayer. Read, listen and chant in the script you read best.

TODAY'S PRACTICE
Four small steps a day: morning prayers, the day's devotion, a few minutes of meditation, and the evening lamp. Tick them off and watch your week fill with rings.

MEDITATE
Choose 1 to 20 minutes, gently close your eyes, and breathe. A gentle vibration guides your breath: it swells as you breathe in and fades as you breathe out, so you never need to look. Meditate to a tanpura drone, a singing bowl, silence, or your own favourite devotional song from your phone. A soft bell tells you when it's time.

YOUR PRACTICE
Your streak, days of prayer, minutes meditated, and a calendar of every day you showed up.

DAILY ROUTINES
Morning prayers, before study, before meals, the evening lamp and before sleep: 17 short mantras, including the Gayatri and Mahamrityunjaya, each with its meaning.

STOTRAS AND AARTI
Hanuman Chalisa, Aditya Hrudayam, Ganesha Pancharatnam, Sri Suktam, Narayana Suktam and Om Jai Jagadish Hare.

LISTEN
Hear any prayer read aloud with your phone's Hindi voice, line by line, with the words highlighted as you go. Works offline. Choose a slow, calm or normal pace.

108 NAMES
The Ashtottara Shatanamavali of Ganesha, Shiva, Lakshmi and Saraswati. Chant one name at a time inside a mala of 108 beads, or turn on Listen and chant along hands-free.

SIX WAYS TO READ
English · IAST · देवनागरी · తెలుగు · ಕನ್ನಡ · ગુજરાતી

MADE FOR EVERY EYE
Large, clear text that follows your phone's font size, TalkBack support, and a dark mode that's easy on the eyes at dawn or night.

PRIVATE
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Everything stays on your device.
