Help test our apps

Pick the apps that interest you, join their tester group with your Gmail, then install from Google Play. It takes about a minute and you don't need to send us anything.

1. Join the groupUse the Gmail that's on your Android phone.
2. Open the test linkTap “Become a tester” on your phone.
3. Install & use itKeep it installed for 14 days and tell us what you think.

We use your Gmail address only to give you access to the test, through Google Groups. We don't see anyone else's address and other testers can't see yours. You can leave the group any time. Android phones only. iPhone testing is by TestFlight, so email us if you'd like an invite.
