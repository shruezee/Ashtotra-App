Privacy Policy

Ashtotra · Effective October 8, 2026 (updated for satsang)

Ashtotra helps you read, listen to and chant daily prayers and the 108 sacred names. It does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Ashtotra has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network connections, and we do not run any servers.

What stays on your device

Ashtotra saves only these, on your device: your chosen script and settings, your favourites, where you stopped in each list, the days you prayed and meditated, and how many times you have completed each list. If you use iCloud backups, they may be included in your own backup, which Apple controls. Deleting the app removes everything.

Read aloud and reminders

Read aloud uses your device's built-in voice, on the device. If you turn on the daily reminder, Ashtotra schedules a local notification on your device; it is not sent through any server.

Your devotional song

If you pick a song from Files for meditation, Ashtotra keeps a private copy on your device so it plays offline; choosing another song replaces it, and deleting the app removes it. If you pick from your Music library instead, Ashtotra asks permission to access it so it can play the song you pick. It only remembers which song you chose, on your device, and never reads, uploads or changes anything else in your library.

Satsang together (SharePlay)

When you start or join a satsang, Ashtotra uses Apple's SharePlay to keep everyone in the FaceTime call or Messages group on the same prayer, video, PDF or image. What the host shares (and the host's place in it) travels between participants' devices through Apple's SharePlay service, end-to-end encrypted. It never passes through any server of ours, and we can't see it. Satsang chat messages and reactions travel the same way, are seen only by people in that satsang, and disappear when it ends. Reporting a message opens an email to us that you can review before sending. Only share files and links you have the right to share.

Purchases

If an optional "Ashtotra Plus" subscription is offered in future, it is handled entirely by Apple. We don't receive your payment details; the app only checks with Apple whether the subscription is active on your device. To know when your free day of hosting or free month of meditation began, the app stores those two dates in your device's Keychain. They never leave your device.

Children

Ashtotra is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
