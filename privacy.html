Privacy Policy

Ashtotra · Effective October 6, 2026

Ashtotra helps you read and chant the 108 sacred names. It does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Ashtotra has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network connections, and we do not run any servers.

What stays on your device

Ashtotra saves only these, on your device: your chosen script and settings, where you stopped in each list, and how many times you have completed each list. If you use iCloud backups, they may be included in your own backup, which Apple controls. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Ashtotra is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
